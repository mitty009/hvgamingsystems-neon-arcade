Questions venue operators ask

What makes a machine commercial rather than domestic?

Commercial machines need to suit repeated public use, venue presentation, support access and the space available for guests, staff and walkways.

Can you recommend the right commercial arcade machine mix?

Yes. The recommendation can include arcade cabinets, racing machines, prize attractions, pool tables and short-play games based on the venue and audience.
