Questions venue operators ask

What arcade machines work well in pubs and clubs?

Racing games, pool tables, prize machines and short-play cabinets are usually strong options because they are visible, social and easy for guests to understand.

Can you help avoid overcrowding the room?

Yes. Machine recommendations can be matched to walkways, sight lines, supervision and the way guests already move through the venue.
